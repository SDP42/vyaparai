Diwali Stocking Plan

Start ordering dry fruits, sweets boxes and gift hampers four weeks before Dhanteras. Ghee, besan, sugar and maida sell two times faster in the ten days before Diwali. Keep extra stock of diyas, candles and decorative lights near the front of the shop.

Last year dry fruit sales jumped threefold in the week of Dhanteras and we ran out on day three. This year order 40 percent more cashew and almonds and reserve a second shelf.

Gift hampers should be packed at least five days before Diwali. Use the Rs 499, Rs 999 and Rs 1,499 price points that sold best.
