GST Filing Notes

We file GSTR-1 by the 11th of every month and GSTR-3B by the 20th. The accountant needs the sales register, the purchase register and the credit notes by the 5th.

Most packaged food is taxed at 5 percent or 12 percent. Loose unbranded grains are exempt. Cold drinks and aerated water attract 28 percent plus cess. Branded atta and rice in packs above 25 kg are taxed at 5 percent.

Input tax credit can be claimed only if the supplier has filed their return and the invoice appears in GSTR-2B. Keep the GSTIN of every supplier on the purchase bill.

Composition scheme is not suitable for us because we sell to registered businesses.
