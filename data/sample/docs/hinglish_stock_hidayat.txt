Stock aur Order ke Niyam

Har Monday subah stock ginti karni hai aur sheet mein likhni hai. Jis item ka stock 5 din se kam bacha ho, uska order usi din dena hai. Atta, chawal, tel aur chini ka kam se kam 10 din ka stock hamesha rakhna hai.

Supplier ko order WhatsApp par bhejna hai aur confirmation screenshot save karna hai. Maal aane par bill se milana hai: quantity, rate aur expiry date. Jo packet phata ya leak hua ho, uski photo kheench kar usi din supplier ko bhejni hai.

Paisa fasa hua dead stock har mahine ke aakhri hafte mein dekhna hai. Jo item 60 din se nahi bika, uspar offer lagana hai.
