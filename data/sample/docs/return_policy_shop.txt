Store Return and Exchange Policy

Customers may return packaged food items within 3 days if the pack is sealed and the bill is shown. Opened or partly used items cannot be returned, except when the product is found to be spoiled or expired.

Electronics and appliances carry a 7 day replacement window for manufacturing defects. After 7 days the customer should contact the brand service centre; we will help with the paperwork.

Refunds are made in the same mode as payment. UPI refunds reach the customer within 2 working days. Cash refunds are given at the counter. Store credit is offered when the customer has no bill.

Loose items such as rice, sugar and pulses are not returnable once weighed and packed.
