Staff Salary and Leave

Salaries are paid on the 5th of every month by bank transfer. Overtime beyond 9 hours a day is paid at one and a half times the hourly rate. Each staff member gets 12 paid leaves per year and one weekly off, which is Tuesday for the counter staff.

During Diwali week no leave is sanctioned. A bonus of one month's salary is given to staff who have completed one year. Advance salary requests above Rs 5,000 need the owner's approval and are deducted in two instalments.
