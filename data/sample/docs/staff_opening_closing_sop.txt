Opening and Closing Checklist

Opening, 8:30 am. Switch on lights, fridge and the billing computer. Count the cash drawer and note the float of Rs 5,000. Check the freezer temperature; it must be below minus 18 degrees. Dust the shelves and refill the front of the shop.

Closing, 9:30 pm. Print the day's sales summary from the billing system. Count the cash and match it with the summary; write any difference in the register. Put cash above Rs 20,000 in the locker. Switch off all lights except the security light. Lock the shutter and the back door.

Cash difference above Rs 200 must be reported to the owner the same night.
