Shree Ganesh Traders: Supply Terms

Payment terms. Invoices are payable within 21 days of the bill date. A cash discount of 2 percent applies if payment is made within 7 days. Delayed payments beyond 45 days attract interest at 18 percent per year.

Minimum order. The minimum order value for free delivery is Rs 15,000. Orders below this amount carry a delivery charge of Rs 400 within Pune city limits.

Delivery. Orders placed before 12 noon are delivered the next working day. Orders placed after noon are delivered in two working days. Deliveries are not made on Sundays.

Damaged goods. Report damaged or leaking packs within 24 hours of delivery with a photo. Replacement is sent with the next delivery. Credit notes are issued only when replacement stock is not available.
