Udhaar (Credit) Rules

Credit is given only to customers known for more than six months. The limit is Rs 3,000 for regular families and Rs 10,000 for local businesses such as tea stalls and hotels.

Every credit sale must be written in the khata with the date, the items and the customer signature or a WhatsApp confirmation. Payments are recorded the same day.

If a customer has not paid for 30 days, send a polite reminder on WhatsApp. After 60 days stop further credit until the balance is cleared. After 90 days the owner will talk to the customer personally.

Never give credit to a new customer, even for a small amount.
